Fall Detection System

A device that watches an accelerometer for falls and sends an alert over the cellular network within seconds.

Hardware

The STM32 microcontroller sits between the sensor and the radio. It reads the accelerometer, runs the detection logic, and drives the GSM module when it sees a fall.

Detection loop

The firmware repeats the same short loop 200 times a second. Most passes end at the threshold check. Only a reading past the threshold starts an alert.

  1. Read sampleEvery 5 ms
  3. Check thresholdBelow it: keep sampling
  5. Fall detectedReading past the threshold
  7. Send GSM alertDelivered in under 3 s

A 92% true-positive rate means the device caught 92 of every 100 real falls in testing. The next step would be measuring false alarms from everyday motion such as sitting down quickly, then tuning the threshold to balance the two.
